The Marketer

I turned an economics degree into a marketing career.

I wanted to make things that actually reach people. Five-plus years of experience across SEO, content strategy, paid media, and social, working across SaaS products, eCommerce brands, and WordPress ecosystems in the US, UK, and Europe.

Right now I'm leading content and social at E-Warmup, a SaaS email deliverability platform. I'm also Head of Marketing at CieloOps, where I co-founded Serpely, an agentic SEO tool built for the AI search era and a product I hold equity in.

Before this, I led digital marketing, content strategy, and social media for international software development companies including WellDev AG (Switzerland) and WPManageNinja, across their product lines and global markets. That's where I learned to build content that ranks, converts, and compounds.

Economics grad from SUST who ended up writing landing pages and building growth funnels. It makes more sense than it sounds.

The Musician

I picked up a guitar in school and never really put it down.

I'm the guitarist, lyricist, and band leader of BUFFERING, an alternative progressive rock and metal band I've been building since 2015. I write and tune all the songs. Three originals out, a debut album on the way, and all our music is live on global platforms including Spotify and Apple Music.

I also play guitar for DOPAMINE, a modern metal band formed through a national battle of the bands reality show. We aired on national TV and finished in the top 3 of The Cage: Season 1. My third band is Eden's Garden, an atmospheric rock project with a sound as cinematic as it gets.

My work extends beyond the bands. As a music producer and session guitarist, I've worked across recording sessions, mixed and mastered tracks from scratch, especially for my bands, and played guitar for a movie score and TVCs and jingles. Sound is a craft, and I take it seriously in every room I work in.

Outside the stage, I've spent years teaching guitar, working with over 20 students throughout my musical journey.
